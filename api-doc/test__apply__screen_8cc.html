◆ test_exchange_geometry()

int test_exchange_geometry ( World & world,
const real_convolution_3d & P,
const double thresh )

T6: the exchange's own geometry. The source is the product of the multiplier with itself, as in the diagonal product bra_i vf_i of the exchange (vf = bra = ket), so the multiplier is O(1) on top of the source at every level of the source. Two multipliers: a Gaussian, whose tree stops at level 5, and a cusp, refined to level 13 at the nucleus, where a box's volume is far below one bohr^3.
