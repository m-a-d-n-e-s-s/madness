Detailed Description

Functor for evaluating the Coulomb potential of all nuclei of a molecule; nuclei are represented by primitive spherical (l=0) Gaussians.

It computes the Gaussian potential generated by a given molecule at a specified position.

DOI 10.1006/adnd.1997.0751
