populations of the intrinsic atomic orbitals (Knizia, J. Chem. Theory Comput. 9, 4834 (2013))

For a complete large basis (MRA) the IAOs follow from S and X of a minimal basis alone: W = X^T S^-1 X, D = S^-1 X W^-1/2 (the depolarized orbitals chi D) M = 1 - D D^T S, K = X^T (2 D D^T S - 1) A = chi M + phi K (the IAOs), <A|A> = M^T S M + M^T X K + K^T X^T M + K^T K, <A|phi> = M^T X + K^T Q = <A|A>^-1/2 <A|phi>, N_rho = sum_i n_i Q_rho,i^2 The orbitals lie in the IAO span, so every column of Q has unit norm and the electrons sum to sum_i n_i to the precision of the overlaps. Throws when W is singular: the minimal basis then misses an orbital's atomic character.

Mulliken or Loewdin populations of the occupied space projected onto the basis.

The fit of phi_i onto the basis is C_i = S^-1 X_i; the fitted orbitals overlap as W = C^T S C = X^T S^-1 X, whose diagonal c_i <= 1 is each orbital's completeness. The fits are orthonormalized among themselves, Cbar = C W^-1/2 (the depolarized orbitals of the IAO construction), so the electrons sum to sum_i n_i and what the basis misses shows as the spilling instead: mulliken: N_mu = sum_i n_i (Cbar_i o S Cbar_i)_mu lowdin: N_mu = sum_i n_i ((S^1/2 Cbar_i)_mu)^2 Both are invariant under rotations among equally occupied orbitals. (Renormalizing each fit by its own c_i also gives the sum rule, but not that invariance.)
