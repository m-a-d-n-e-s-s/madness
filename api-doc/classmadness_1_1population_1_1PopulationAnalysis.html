Detailed Description

the population analysis of the population keys, for orbitals given in real space

For each scheme of the population key: reads the basis set by name (population_basis for mulliken and lowdin, population_minbasis for iao), projects it at the current FunctionDefaults, forms S and X, and prints a table per scheme on rank 0 at print_level > 0. The occupied orbitals are those with a nonzero occupation, so explicit holes count, and the total charge is the nuclear charge minus their sum. The schemes assume orthonormal orbitals; after an SCF they are so only to its screening and truncation, so they are orthonormalized first (Loewdin, orthonormalize_symmetric). A failing scheme only warns: its entry in the result holds the error. moldft (SCF::population_analysis) and nemo (Nemo::population_analysis) call it after the final rung.
